# Where to apply for your permit

San Francisco offers multiple ways to apply for a permit based on your project.

Department of Building Inspection

Start by identifying the permit type your project requires. Then choose the process that applies to your project: PermitSF Portal, Contractor Permits, Over-the-Counter or In-House Review.

## Apply in the PermitSF Portal

Apply in the PermitSF portal for the following projects:

- Electrical permits without plans
- Plumbing and mechanical permits without plans
- In-kind, nonstructural kitchen or bath work for eligible residential buildings
- Reroofing without plans
- Signs and awnings (new install and legalization)
- Door replacement (in-kind)
- Window or skylight replacement (in-kind)
- Exterior siding repair or replacement (in-kind)
- Fire alarms
- Fire sprinklers
- Water flow information requests

You will need to create a free account, then apply through the PermitSF portal.

More permit types will be added to the PermitSF portal in the coming months.

## Apply for Contractor Permits online

Apply online for permits that are issued immediately and do not require plans. You must be a registered contractor to use this option.

Apply for a Contractor Permit online for projects such as:

- Solar photovoltaic system
- Permit to Operate Boiler (PTO)

## Apply for Over-the-Counter permits at the Permit Center

Apply in person at the Permit Center for projects that can be reviewed the same day or within a short timeframe. Over-the-Counter permits may require plans depending on the scope of work.

Over-the-Counter permits without plans include, but are not limited to:

- In-kind repair decks and/or stairs (less than 50%)
- In-kind minor dry rot repair
- In-kind replacement of garage doors (same size and same location)
- Exploratory work to determine depth of footing, stud size/spacing, floor/roof joist size and direction. (Holes to be filled with slurry, and finishes to be replaced with the same fire resistance rating)
- Replacement of wall finishes in R-3 occupancies complying with SFEBC Section 503.11.1
- Bolting down existing foundation and/or adding plywood panels to crawl space/1st floor garage area (not complying with DBI Information Sheet S-09)

Over-the-Counter permits with plans include, but are not limited to:

- Roofing replacement
- Kitchen remodel (changing layout, floor plan or removing walls)
- Bathroom remodel (changing layout, floor plan or removing walls)
- Residential interior remodel (changing floor plans or removing walls)
- Repairing decks and/or stairs (more than 50%)
- New windows and/or exterior doors in new locations
- New skylights in new locations
- Decks less than 20 feet above grade that meet Planning Department setbacks
- Single story non-structural commercial tenant improvement projects
- Permits to comply with accessibility requirements
- Installation of power door operators
- Voluntary structural strengthening
- Disability access barrier removal projects
- Small to medium projects that do not require Planning Department neighborhood notification
- Fences taller than 6 feet in side or rear yard and/or fences taller than 3 feet in front yard
- Exterior façade work, visible from street, that involves changes in finishes (for example, stucco to siding, wood to vinyl siding, and others)
- New mechanical equipment installed inside or outside the building
- Storefront security gates (excluding some preservation districts)
- Residential security gates at entry doors and/or window security bars, grills, and/or grates
- Replacement of wall finishes in occupancies other than R-3 occupancies
- New washer, dryer, and/or laundry sink hookups in new/existing areas within the existing building
- In-kind replacement of garage slab (no ceiling height changes) although note that saw-cutting SOG to repair/replace sewer lines does not require plans
- Brace and bolt per DBI Information Sheet S-09
- Removal of Underground Storage Tanks

## Apply for In-House Review permits online

Submit your application for plan review if your project is larger, more complex, or requires detailed review. Plans are required.

Apply for In-House Review online for:

- Projects that require Department of Public Health Site Mitigation Program review, including work in areas of known or suspected contamination (and additional criteria) or any dust-generating work (demolition, earthwork, construction) on parcels greater than 0.5 acres
- Change of use tenant improvements that involve Family Day Care, “E” or "I" occupancies, and Laboratory "L" uses
- Serial permitting that continues to increase the scope of the initial remodel
- Foundation replacements and/or seismic upgrades when there is a prior permit, for further expansion, under review
- Projects located in hillside or landslide areas that may require a geotechnical report, third-party engineering review, or Structural Advisory Committee review, including properties in earthquake-induced landslide zones or within the Northwest Mount Sutro or Edgehill Mountain Slope Protection Areas
- Electric Vehicle Charging Locations (EVCLs)
- Excavation projects that expands the below grade footprint
- Extensive residential remodels that changes the gravity load carrying members
- Extensive residential remodels that requires a mandatory seismic upgrade on all levels
- Special (non-conventional) foundation systems including but not limited to: tie backs, deep foundations, and/or complex shoring systems
- Extensive commercial tenant improvements that changes the gravity load carrying members
- Tower Crane installation permits
- Adding and/or removal of unit
- ADU or unit legalization
- Advertising Billboard removal
- Change of use from partial restaurant to full restaurant
- Housing projects
- MOU projects
- Revision permit to remove sprinkler from Issued Building Permit
- Revision to Site permit
- Creation and/or legalization of one or more housing units
